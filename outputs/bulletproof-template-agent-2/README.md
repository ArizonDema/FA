# Challenging Direct-Method Cash Flow Template

Created for analyzer testing. The workbook contains a 2026 monthly direct-method cash flow layout with writable zero-input rows, calculated subtotal rows, opening cash, monthly cash movement, and closing cash.

## Row Meanings

- Opening cash on hand and at bank: Cash available at the start of each month.
- Receipts: settlement-lagged trade takings: Cash collected from customer or platform activity after settlement delays.
- Receipts: retainer drawdowns released: Cash released from customer deposits, prepayments, or retainers as related work is performed.
- Receipts: merchant service rebates cleared: Cash received from processor rebates, vendor incentives, or fee refunds.
- Receipts: insurance recovery cash: Cash received from insurance claims or similar reimbursements.
- Operating cash gathered: Total operating cash received during the period.
- Payments: rostered crew disbursements: Cash paid to employees, contractors, or similar labor groups.
- Payments: premises and yard occupancy: Cash paid for occupied property such as offices, warehouses, storage yards, or operating sites.
- Payments: utility and connectivity clearings: Cash paid for utilities, telecommunications, internet, and related operating services.
- Payments: stock replenishment wires: Cash paid to replenish inventory, materials, goods for resale, or operating supplies.
- Payments: freight, duties, and landing charges: Cash paid to transport goods and clear shipping, import, or customs costs.
- Payments: tax authority sweeps: Cash paid to government authorities for tax-related obligations.
- Payments: claims, refunds, and make-good credits: Cash paid to customers or counterparties for returns, refunds, claims, credits, or service remedies.
- Payments: professional bench and licenses: Cash paid for advisors, subscriptions, permits, dues, software, and operating licenses.
- Operating cash applied: Total operating cash paid out during the period.
- Net cash from day-to-day trading: Operating cash gathered less operating cash applied.
- Equipment refresh and fit-out checks: Cash spent on longer-lived assets, equipment, technology, or facility improvements.
- Proceeds from retired kit and fixtures: Cash received from selling used equipment, fixtures, vehicles, or other long-lived assets.
- Investment in pledged term deposits: Cash placed into restricted deposits or other reserves that are not freely available.
- Release of pledged term deposits: Cash released from restricted deposits or reserves back into available cash.
- Net cash from asset and reserve moves: Net cash effect of asset purchases, asset sales, and reserve deposit changes.
- Borrowing draws booked at treasury: Cash received from new borrowing or drawdowns on financing facilities.
- Lender principal retirements: Cash paid to reduce borrowing principal.
- Member capital subscriptions banked: Cash contributed by owners, members, shareholders, or partners.
- Partner preference redemptions paid: Cash paid to redeem or return capital interests to owners or investors.
- Net cash from funding bench: Net cash effect of borrowings, repayments, and owner capital flows.
- Bank fees, rounding, and timing wash: Small cash differences from bank fees, rounding, exchange differences, or timing adjustments.
- Total period cash movement: Net change in cash for the month after all sections and adjustments.
- Closing cash on hand and at bank: Cash available at the end of each month.

## Notes

- Yellow cells in the workbook are writable inputs and are initialized to zero.
- Gray cells are formulas.
- Monthly columns cover January through December 2026, with an annual summary column.
- The labels are intentionally plausible but non-standard to make analyzer matching more challenging.
