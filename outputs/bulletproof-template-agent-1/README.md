# Deliberately Challenging Direct-Method Cash Flow Template

This folder contains one direct-method cash flow workbook for calendar year 2026. Monthly writable input rows start at zero. Formula rows calculate subtotals, monthly net movement, and closing cash.

## Row meanings

- **Opening cash - bank ledger brought forward**: Cash available at the start of the month before the month's cash activity. January is an input; later months link from the prior month's closing cash.
- **Marketplace sweep receipts**: Cash collected from marketplace, card, or platform settlement batches after processors release funds.
- **Invoice lockbox lodgements**: Cash received against issued invoices or trade accounts through lockbox or bank deposit channels.
- **Retainer and milestone releases**: Previously agreed customer deposits, retainers, or milestone amounts that become available as cash.
- **Duty refunds and authority paybacks**: Cash received back from tax authorities, customs, grant bodies, or similar public agencies.
- **Vendor credits cashed back**: Supplier rebates, cash refunds, claims recoveries, or credit balances returned in cash.
- **Subtotal - operating money in**: Total operating cash received during the month.
- **Merchandise settlement batches**: Cash paid to acquire inventory, materials, or other goods used in operations.
- **Crew compensation wires and levies**: Cash paid for employee compensation, employment taxes, benefits, and related workforce obligations.
- **Premises service charges and utilities**: Cash paid for facilities, property services, maintenance, energy, and similar occupancy needs.
- **Carriage, fulfillment, and route costs**: Cash paid for shipping, logistics, field travel, delivery, freight, postage, or fulfillment activity.
- **Channel tolls, commissions, and merchant fees**: Cash paid for sales channels, referral commissions, payment processing, and transaction platforms.
- **Policy cover, permits, and adviser retainers**: Cash paid for insurance cover, licenses, regulatory filings, legal, accounting, or advisory support.
- **Collected taxes passed through**: Cash remitted to tax authorities for amounts collected on behalf of others, such as sales tax or VAT.
- **Subtotal - operating money out**: Total operating cash paid during the month.
- **Cash surplus from trading pulse**: Operating receipts less operating payments.
- **Workshop kit and fit-out purchases**: Cash paid for durable equipment, shop-floor assets, fixtures, or site improvements.
- **Internal platform build disbursements**: Cash paid for internally used systems, software development, infrastructure, or implementation work.
- **Retired asset proceeds and warranty recoveries**: Cash received from selling old assets, insurance proceeds, or warranty claims.
- **Net cash from asset-cycle traffic**: Cash recovered from assets less cash paid for asset and platform investment.
- **Fresh lender advances received**: Borrowed cash received from lenders or credit facilities.
- **Scheduled note paydowns**: Cash paid to reduce loan principal or similar borrowing balances.
- **Shareholder funding calls banked**: Cash received from owners, members, or shareholders as funding contributions.
- **Member preference cash redemptions**: Cash returned to investors, members, or holders of preference-like capital claims.
- **Net cash from funding traffic**: Financing cash received less financing cash repaid or returned.
- **Net cash movement for the month**: Combined monthly effect of operating, asset-cycle, and funding cash activity.
- **Closing cash - bank ledger carried forward**: Ending cash after adding monthly net cash movement to opening cash.
